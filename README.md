# Cute Clock

A tiny always-on-top desktop clock + countdown timer with three looks: a unicorn, a bowl of ramen and a volcano. Windows only, nothing to install.

## Run
`git clone <repo-url>`, then double-click **BEST_CLOCK_EVAAAA.bat**.

## Use
- **Drag** anywhere to move it; **drag the bottom-right corner** (shows on hover) to resize
- **Clock and timer buttons** (top right, round) switch between the clock and the timer
- **Look button** (left of the clock button) cycles unicorn → ramen → volcano. It shows an icon of the look you'll get next.
- **Timer:** click the time to type one (`25` = minutes, `5:30`, `1h30m`, or `+5` to add), or use -1 / +1 / +5, mouse wheel, play/pause, reset
- The timer keeps running in the background while you view the clock. The bar at the bottom slowly fills as time passes (gold for the unicorn, red for ramen, molten orange for the volcano), and the alarm chimes 3 times at zero, then keeps flashing until you tap it.
- **Right-click** for 24h / seconds / date / always-on-top / themes / quit. The colour themes apply to the unicorn look; ramen and volcano have their own colours.

## Looks
- **Unicorn:** the original purple card with a bobbing unicorn and twinkling sparkles.
- **Ramen:** a light yellow card with a top view of a wooden bowl of boiling spicy broth. Noodles arrive after 1/4 of the timer, mushrooms and scallions after 2/4, the rest of the toppings after 3/4, and a pair of chopsticks lands in the bowl when the alarm rings.
- **Volcano:** blue sky over dark brown ground. Lava slowly flows down the volcano as the timer runs, and when the alarm rings it erupts with rocks and lava while the sky turns grey. In clock mode the lava is shown fully flowing.

Position, size and settings are remembered (stored in %APPDATA%\cute-clock).
